# Acceptance Tests

## A01 Valid input
LOCKED ProductDNA + lock spec → READY_FOR_CONCEPTS.

## A02 Invalid input
Missing primary product → reject.

## A03 Candidate count
Generate 3–5 candidates.

## A04 Candidate diversity
Candidates differ in style/objective logic, not adjective only.

## A05 Product mutation blocked
Concept changing collar → rejected/rewrite.

## A06 Real accessory preserved
Confirmed straw hat stays preserved.

## A07 Unsupported accessory
Concept adding bag not in DNA → blocked in strict mode.

## A08 No camera leakage
Output CreativeBrief has no focal length/camera movement.

## A09 No motion leakage
No exact action timeline.

## A10 No cinematic leakage
No lighting ratio/DOF/color-grade instruction.

## A11 Unsupported claim
No “100% cotton” if DNA does not confirm.

## A12 Broad audience
No sensitive demographic inference.

## A13 Score dimensions
Each candidate has all score fields.

## A14 Compatibility check
Cannot LOCK until ProductDNA locks compatible.

## A15 Handoff
T03→T04 envelope schema-valid.

## A16 Immutable
Locked brief edited → revision, not in-place mutation.

## A17 Product emphasis
Primary product hero attributes preserved.

## A18 Fashion-commercial slider
Changing balance updates creative brief intent only.
