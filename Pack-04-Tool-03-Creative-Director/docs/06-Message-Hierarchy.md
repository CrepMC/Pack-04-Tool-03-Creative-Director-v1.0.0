# 06 — Message Hierarchy

Creative Director có thể xác định message intent nhưng không cần viết caption final.

## Layers

Hero message:
- “The top is the clear visual hero.”

Supporting:
- “Outfit feels polished and wearable.”

Proof point:
- “Pattern, neckline and drape remain readable.”

CTA intent:
- “Encourage product consideration / affiliate click.”

Avoid:
- unsupported claims về chất liệu, giá, brand, sustainability, body effect.

Không invent claim “100% cotton” từ ProductDNA material appearance.
