# 03 — Concept Candidate System

Tool nên tạo 3–5 concept candidates khác biệt thật sự.

Mỗi candidate gồm:
- concept_id;
- title;
- one-line thesis;
- objective;
- tone;
- product emphasis;
- audience intent;
- platform fit;
- commercial/fashion balance;
- strengths;
- risks;
- product lock compatibility;
- score dimensions.

Không tạo 5 concept chỉ khác vài adjective.

## Candidate diversity

Ví dụ:
1. Clean Commercial Showcase
2. Premium Editorial Product Focus
3. Social-First Fashion Demo
4. Natural Lifestyle Styling
5. Runway Confidence Product Readability

Mỗi concept phải khác về **commercial logic**, không chỉ mood.
