# 11 — UI Specification

## Regions

- Upstream Product Identity card.
- Campaign Intent controls.
- Concept candidate gallery.
- Compare view.
- Selected concept editor.
- Product emphasis hierarchy.
- Message hierarchy.
- Creative risk panel.

## Candidate card

Hiển thị:
- title;
- thesis;
- objective;
- tone;
- product focus;
- platform fit;
- scores;
- risk badges.

## Compare

Cho phép compare 2–3 concept side-by-side.
Không để một score tổng duy nhất che mất risk.
